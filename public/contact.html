3. Response Expectations

We strive to respond to all inquiries promptly and thoroughly. Here is what you can expect after reaching out to us:

  • General questions: Response within 24-48 hours during business days (Monday through Friday).
  • Bug reports: Acknowledgment within 24 hours. Resolution timeline depends on complexity — simple formatting bugs are typically fixed within 48 hours; complex parser issues may take up to 7 days.
  • Feature suggestions: Acknowledgment within 48 hours. We review all suggestions during our quarterly planning cycles and will notify you if your suggestion is selected for implementation.
  • Privacy/data requests: Acknowledgment within 24 hours. Full response within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA) as required by applicable regulation.
  • Business inquiries: Response within 3-5 business days, depending on the nature and complexity of the proposal.
  • Media inquiries: Response within 48 hours. Time-sensitive press requests should include "PRESS" in the email subject line for prioritized handling.

Our business hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM (UTC+0). Emails received outside business hours or on weekends and public holidays will be addressed on the next business day. We do not provide phone support at this time — email is our sole communication channel to ensure all inquiries receive thorough, documented responses.

4. How to Contact Us Effectively

To help us resolve your inquiry as quickly as possible, please include the following information in your email when applicable:

  • Subject line: A clear, descriptive subject line helps us route your email to the right team member. Example: "Table formatting broken in DOCX export" is much more helpful than "Help needed."
  • Detailed description: Describe your question, issue, or suggestion in as much detail as possible. For bug reports, explain what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • Steps to reproduce: For bugs, provide the exact steps to reproduce the issue — including the specific Markdown text that caused the problem (paste it directly in the email).
  • Browser and OS: Include your browser name and version (e.g., Chrome 120, Firefox 115) and operating system (e.g., Windows 11, macOS Sonoma, Ubuntu 22.04).
  • Export format: Specify which export format (PDF, DOCX, DOC, HTML) was being used when the issue occurred.
  • Screenshots: If the issue is visual, screenshots comparing the preview with the exported file are extremely helpful for our debugging process.
